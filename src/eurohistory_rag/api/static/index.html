    How to read these numbers

    
        Sixty questions, each with the correct answer written down by hand
    beforehand. The system sits the exam, the paper is kept, and these are the
    marks. Two different things are being marked:
    did it find the right passage, and was the answer any good.
    A system can be excellent at one and hopeless at the other.
        

    
        The one number that matters most: recall@20 is far
    higher than recall@5. The right passage is nearly always retrieved — it is
    just not always in the first five. That means the weakness is
    ordering, not searching. The book is on the trolley; it is not in the
    first five you pick up.
        

    
        Why the table is split by kind
        
    
        An overall average hides opposite movements. Easy questions carry it up
    and reworded ones drag it down, so a change that helps one and hurts the
    other looks like no change at all. arts is how many different
    articles the five passages came from — five slots holding one article three
    times is five slots doing the work of three.
        

    
        Why some cells say n/a
        
    
        The unanswerable questions have no correct answer on file, because
    nothing should come back. Scoring them 0% would read as failure when
    refusing is exactly right, so they are marked n/a and greyed in the strip
    below.
        

    
        Golden, extended, all
        
    
        The first thirty questions have never been edited since Phase 7, so
    every run since is comparable to every other. The second thirty were added
    in Phase 15 to cover the parts of the corpus the first thirty never asked
    about. They are scored separately because mixing them would break the
    comparison the first thirty exist to provide.
        

    
        When a change counts
        
    
        The same run repeated three times does not give the same answer numbers.
    Measured in Phase 16: a generation result has to move by more than
    4 unsupported claims, 0.7 faithfulness points or
    2 fully-faithful answers before it means anything. Retrieval and
    refusal numbers are exact — any change in those is real.
    Weigh yourself three times on the same scales and you
    get three numbers. Anything smaller than that spread is the scales, not
    you.
